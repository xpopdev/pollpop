argument-hint: '[idea name, candidate number, or path to the idea''s entry]'

Delegate to the contrarian agent to attack $ARGUMENTS (or, if no argument was given, the current top-ranked candidate in `company/research/opportunity_map.md`).

The contrarian agent should genuinely try to answer, against the idea:
- Why will this fail?
- Why won't users care?
- Why won't they pay?
- Why will competitors copy it?
- Why is the market smaller than it looks?
- Why can't an incumbent just build this?
- What hidden dependency does the idea rely on?
- What regulatory or technical problem hasn't been addressed?
- What's the single weakest assumption underneath it?
- What evidence would we need that we don't currently have?

Record the attack directly against the candidate's entry in `company/research/opportunity_map.md` (or in a dedicated file under `company/research/` for a deeper critique), and make sure the result reaches the ceo agent before any product-selection decision gets written to `company/decisions/approved.md`. If the idea survives, state plainly what nearly killed it and why it didn't — a survived attack with no real near-misses recorded is a sign the attack wasn't serious enough, not that the idea is unusually strong.
