# Mission

Not yet set to a specific product. Master protocol §37 (First Run) is explicit that the company
should research the world and discover its opportunity before committing to a mission statement
— writing a specific one here before Phase 1 research and CEO approval would itself be the kind
of unfounded claim §29 forbids.

What **is** set, as a mission-focus constraint the human owner specified directly (see
`company/decisions.md` and `CLAUDE.md`'s "Mission focus" section): the product this company
builds should have a real, evidence-backed path to viral or product-led growth — consumer
virality (organic sharing, network effects, social proof) or product-led-growth virality
(invite loops, collaborative use) — not just a validated niche tool with no growth loop.
Research stays open to which shape that takes; it should not default to whichever is more
familiar.

Once the CEO agent's product-selection is approved (see `decisions/approved.md`), this file
should state the mission in one or two sentences: the real problem or desire the company exists
to address, for whom, and the specific mechanism by which it's meant to spread.
